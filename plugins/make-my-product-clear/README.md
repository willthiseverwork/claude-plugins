# Make My Product Clear

![Make My Product Clear icon](assets/icon.svg)

Drop in a product page, app listing, description, or screenshots. Get one rewrite that makes the customer's reason to care and next action clear while keeping claims and offer terms tied to your source. App Store search fields wait for keyword evidence.

## Install in Claude Code

```text
/plugin marketplace add willthiseverwork/claude-plugins
/plugin install make-my-product-clear@willthiseverwork
```

Start a new Claude Code session after installation.

## What it does

- Rewrite existing product pages
- Rewrite app listing descriptions
- Clarify the customer's next action

You get one recommended draft in the order of your page, with a short diagnosis of the customer's situation, desired progress and the next commitment the page asks for. Facts, prices, trial and refund terms and other offer conditions stay as your source states them. Claims that need confirmation are listed separately instead of being written into publishable copy.

## How to use it

Paste the copy, share a public URL, or attach a file or screenshots, and say who it is for if you know. For example:

- "Drop in my product page and rewrite it so customers see why it matters and what to do next."
- "Rewrite my App Store description and screenshot lines. Hold title and subtitle until we have search terms."
- "Rewrite this product description around the customer's situation, desired progress, and first decision."

For App Store listings, the app name, subtitle and keyword field are search fields: the plugin keeps them as labelled references until you supply keyword evidence, and rewrites the description and screenshot story.

## What is inside

The `make-my-product-clear` skill and one reference file with guidance for App Store listings, product and landing pages, and product or commerce descriptions.

## Data and privacy

The plugin is instructions only. It has no server, connector, account, database or telemetry, and it sends nothing to Will This Ever Work. Claude processes the copy, files and screenshots you provide under your Claude account's terms and privacy settings. If you give a public URL, Claude may retrieve that page to complete your request. Provide product-facing material only; redact customer records or other personal data that appear in screenshots.

Privacy notice and support: https://www.willthiseverwork.com/plugins/privacy/ (support email: support@willthiseverwork.com).

## Limits

A rewrite is not measured conversion lift. Editing your store listing or website and publishing changes stay with you.
