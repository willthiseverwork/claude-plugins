# Surface guidance

Read the section matching the requested asset. Preserve the source's actual structure when that is more useful than the outline below.

## App Store listing

Treat the listing as a sequence: name, subtitle, screenshot captions/order, any preview, promotional text, description, and version notes when a real update exists. Rewrite the description and screenshot story around one customer job and the next action. Inspect actual images before claiming a caption fits a screen; if images are unavailable, call the captions provisional concepts. Do not invent a feature, UI state, promotional announcement, or version change.

The app name, subtitle, and private keyword field are also search fields. Finalize them only with relevant App Store search-intent evidence for the storefront and language; a persuasive line alone is not sufficient. If no keyword data exists, place these fields outside the recommended copy. Label their current values as references only, state what research is pending, and flag any unsupported or vague claim already present. Do not advise continued publication of a flagged claim merely because the metadata rewrite is on hold; identify it as a separate review before the next release. Proceed with the description and screenshots. If the separate **ASO Keyword Intent** classifier is available, its output can filter supplied raw terms; it does not fetch them or prove volume, organic ranking, or installs. Google Keyword Planner data is optional and is not App Store search volume. Apple Ads data requires authorized account access, not an anonymous API call. Do not connect an account or create an ad campaign just to rewrite copy.

Check current Apple metadata limits and policies before making a publication-ready package. For health or finance apps, distinguish self-monitoring or planning from professional guidance and verify material access or subscription terms.

## Product or landing page

Keep the page's actual audience, offer, calls to action, proof, and necessary terms. A useful sequence often moves from the customer's situation and desired progress to the mechanism, specific evidence, the decision or tradeoff, and the next action. Do not force that order when the existing page has a better one. Rewrite all requested sections, including supporting lines and CTA labels, so they read as one page rather than unrelated slogans.

If the page asks for sign-up, a trial, a demo, payment, or sensitive data, make the exchange and first step clear. Preserve limitations, eligibility, price, and trial terms. Recommend a flow repair separately if the page cannot make an inaccurate or broken offer credible.

## Product description or commerce page

Connect the use situation and desired result to the product's real materials, dimensions, compatibility, care, contents, and purchase choices. Keep specifications easy to find. Do not invent performance tests, certification, sustainability impact, sensory experience, stock urgency, delivery promises, or return terms. If a variant choice, size guide, or compatibility check is the next commitment, make that decision easier before asking for purchase.
