# Job Interview Story Coach

![Job Interview Story Coach icon](assets/icon.svg)

Prepare for an interview in two stages: recover truthful, role-relevant experience into a story bank, then practise one question at a time with focused feedback. It adapts to the role, seniority, interview stage, and business model.

## Install in Claude Code

```text
/plugin marketplace add willthiseverwork/claude-plugins
/plugin install interview-story-coach@willthiseverwork
```

Start a new Claude Code session after installation.

## What it does

- Build interview stories
- Prepare honest gap answers
- Run adaptive mock interviews

Each requirement in the job description is mapped to direct, bounded, adjacent, proposed or missing evidence. The coach never turns a possibility into a fact, keeps your own decisions separate from team results, and helps you answer honestly where experience is missing.

## How to use it

Start with the job description. A CV, application answers, notes, an interview invitation or earlier feedback help but are optional. For example:

- "Use this job description to help me find real examples and prepare interview answers. Ask one question at a time."
- "Use this job description and my CV to identify the experience I should recover for the interview, without inventing anything."
- "Run a hiring-manager mock interview for this role, one question at a time, and give me actionable feedback after each answer."

## What is inside

The `interview-story-coach` skill and three reference files: case building, practice and feedback, and a fictional worked example used only as a format aid.

## Data and privacy

The plugin is instructions only. It has no server, connector, account, database or telemetry, and it sends nothing to Will This Ever Work. Claude processes the job description, CV, notes and practice answers you provide under your Claude account's terms and privacy settings. Share only information you are entitled to share, and leave out credentials, financial or government identifiers and confidential material the task does not need.

Privacy notice and support: https://www.willthiseverwork.com/plugins/privacy/ (support email: support@willthiseverwork.com).

## Limits

This is coaching, not a promise of an interview outcome. It does not research employers, submit applications, contact anyone or make decisions for you.
